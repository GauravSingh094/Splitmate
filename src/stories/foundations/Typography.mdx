# 🔤 Typography & Hierarchy

Splito leverages Vercel's **Geist Sans** and **Geist Mono** typefaces with precise line heights and letter spacing.

---

## Font Families

- **Sans**: `var(--font-geist-sans)` — Used for headlines, UI text, buttons, and navigation.
- **Mono**: `var(--font-geist-mono)` — Used for financial figures, codes, timestamps, and currency numbers.

---

## Scale Reference

| Level | Size | Weight | Tracking | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Heading 1** | `1.875rem (30px)` | Bold (700) | `-0.025em` | Page titles |
| **Heading 2** | `1.5rem (24px)` | Semibold (600) | `-0.02em` | Section headers |
| **Heading 3** | `1.25rem (20px)` | Medium (500) | `-0.015em` | Card titles |
| **Body Base** | `0.875rem (14px)` | Regular (400) | `normal` | Body text |
| **Small** | `0.75rem (12px)` | Regular (400) | `normal` | Captions & Badges |
