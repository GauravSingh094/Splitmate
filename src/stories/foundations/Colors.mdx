# 🎨 Neo-Clay Color System & Tokens

Splito utilizes a curated HSL color token architecture designed for high contrast, accessibility, and sleek dark mode glassmorphism.

---

## Core Brand Palette

- **Primary**: `hsl(var(--primary))` — High vibrancy brand accent.
- **Background**: `hsl(var(--background))` — Deep space dark background `#09090b`.
- **Surface**: `hsl(var(--surface))` — Elevated glass surface container.
- **Muted**: `hsl(var(--muted))` — Subdued neutral surface and borders.
- **Border**: `hsl(var(--border))` — Crisp divider token.

---

## Neo-Clay Elevation Surfaces

1. **Flat Surface (`variant="flat"`)**: Minimalist border container.
2. **Raised Surface (`variant="raised"`)**: Neo-Clay raised shadow backdrop (`shadow-neo-2`).
3. **Interactive Surface (`variant="interactive"`)**: Hover responsive transition with elevated lift (`shadow-neo-3`).
