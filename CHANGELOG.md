## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-01

### Added
- **Core Expense Engine**: Support for Equal, Percentage, Exact Amount, and Shares-based expense splitting.
- **Group & Settlement Management**: Group creation, member management, balance summaries, and settlement recording.
- **Neo-Clay Design Tokens**: Surface elevation tokens, dynamic accent switching, and Framer Motion spring physics.
- **Offline & PWA Queue Engine**: IndexedDB mutation queueing and automatic sync on network reconnection.
- **OpenAPI DTO Integration**: Auto-generated DTO types (`src/generated/api/types.ts`) and Axios client.
- **Enterprise Reporting**: Export capabilities for CSV (UTF-8 BOM), JSON, and browser print rendering.
- **Workspace Personalization**: Custom accent themes (Indigo, Emerald, Purple, Amber, Rose), density modes, and motion toggles.
- **Developer Experience Platform**: Commitlint conventional commits, VS Code workspace settings, GitHub issue/PR templates, and comprehensive documentation manuals.
